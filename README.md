# IntonationTrainer
An app to train recognizing intonation of intervals

Currently has two modes:

------ Intonation Trainer -----

Choose what intervals you want to develop and how off they are allowed to be.  An interval plays and you must identify the distance, as well as if there is a tuning error.

------ Chord Trainer --------

Plays all the voices of a chord and you must identify who is wrong and why!
